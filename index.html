Story

Games are better when you play them standing next to someone. A cabinet in a taproom puts friends round one screen again, with a pint in hand and a high score to beat.

Indybox puts independent games on those cabinets. Venues get something people talk about, and small developers get somewhere real for people to find their work.

For venues

We build the cabinet, wrap it in graphics to suit your place and rent it to you by the month. It sits in your taproom or bar loaded with games from independent developers.

Customers play for free. No coins, no cash to empty. The developers get their game in front of real players, and your regulars get something to stand around and argue over.

  • Built and wrapped to match your venue
  • Free play, no coin mech
  • Indie games from developers we know
  • We install it and look after it

For devs

Make games? We want yours on the cabinets. Players find them in pubs and taprooms, with no store page to scroll past and nothing to download.

We agree licensing with you before any game goes on a cabinet, and you decide whether it stays. Email us a link and a line about your game.

Join the community. Indybox is a growing group of indie developers whose games get played in real venues. Join to hear when new cabinets go live, get your game in the rotation, and meet other devs doing the same.
